A race along a coiled snake, moved by guessing. Each team has one, two or
three pharaohs; the side to move hides 1 to 5 marbles and the other side
guesses how many. Squares run from 1 at the tail to 70 at the head. The
rules in short follow; the full rules, their sources and the history are in
RULES.txt and the rulebook.

Masters 2024
------------

- Two teams, lions and lionesses, each with three cats. All pharaohs start
  off the board beside the tail; a coin picks who goes first.
- Your turn: choose a pharaoh still on its way. You hide 1 to 5 marbles; the
  other team guesses.
- Wrong guess: your pharaoh moves as many squares as you hid.
- Right guess: they move one of their own free pharaohs that many instead,
  and may set a cat on your pharaoh. If all theirs are held, nobody moves,
  but the cat may still be set.
- A held pharaoh must first kill its cat: the cat’s team hides 1 to 3, you
  guess. Right: the cat dies and you take a normal turn. Wrong: your turn
  ends.
- Two right guesses in a row (for a move, not in a cat fight) bring back one
  of the guessing team’s dead cats.
- Ending on an opposing pharaoh: swap; it goes back to the square you came
  from (to the start if you were entering), its cat with it. Ending on a
  teammate: step on one square.
- Reaching or passing square 70 is rebirth; no exact count needed. The first
  team with all its pharaohs reborn wins.

Playing it in the terminal
--------------------------

- 1 to 5 (1 to 3 in a cat fight): hide or guess that many; or Left, Right,
  Up, Down, Tab to choose and Enter or Space to confirm.
- Choosing a pharaoh: arrows or Tab, then Enter; or click it.
- Set a cat? Y or Enter: yes; N: no. The mouse clicks numbers and answers
  too.
- Two people at one keyboard: the guessing team looks away, then Enter.
- After a game: Enter plays again. M: menu; F1: this help; Q: quit.
- O: the ornament off and on (the scales of the serpent’s tail, after the
  alabaster board in Chicago); --plain starts without it.
