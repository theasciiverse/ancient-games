Asciiverse
2026-10-22

Every game here is sown the same way: lift all the seeds of one of your pits
and drop them one by one into the pits that follow. Where the last seed
falls decides what happens next. South’s pits are A, B, C … along the
bottom, North’s a, b, c … along the top, each in the order of sowing. The
rules of each game in short follow; the full rules, their sources and the
history are in RULES.txt and the rulebook.

Oware, Abapa
------------

- 6 pits a side, 4 seeds each. Sow anticlockwise from any pit of yours; 12
  seeds or more pass over the pit they came from.
- Capture: the last seed makes a pit on the other side 2 or 3; take it, and
  the pits before it on that side holding 2 or 3.
- A capture that would leave the other side with nothing takes nothing.
- If the other side has no seeds, you must give it some. If you cannot, the
  game ends and you take your own seeds.
- 25 seeds win; 24 each is a draw. A position seen three times ends the
  game, each side taking its own seeds.

Wari, after Bell
----------------

- As Oware: 6 pits, 4 seeds; captures of 2 or 3 on the other side and back
  along it; 12 seeds or more pass over their own pit.
- A move that would take every seed on the other side may not be played
  (unless every move would).
- Feed a side that has nothing; if you cannot, you take what is left.
- The game ends when a side to move has nothing; the most seeds wins.

Mankal’ah, the game of the ignorant
-----------------------------------

- One player sets out the 72 pebbles unevenly, uncounted; the other may turn
  the board round, and then the setter begins.
- You always play from your last pit, F, or the loaded pit nearest it.
- Where the last pebble falls: making 1, the turn ends; making 2 or 4, take
  it and the pit facing it, and so with each pit before it holding 2 or 4
  (either side); making 3, 5 or more, lift it and sow on.
- A side with none is given one pebble in its first pit; a single pebble
  left belongs to its side.
- A board ends when it is empty: the winner scores the difference. 60 points
  win.

Mankal’ah, the game of the wise
-------------------------------

- As the game of the ignorant, but the pebbles go into matching pits on both
  sides (commonly the four middle pits, or half in A and half in a), and you
  may play from any pit of yours.
- Last pebble making 1 ends the turn; 2 or 4 captures with the pits facing;
  3, 5 or more sows on. 60 points win.

Kalah
-----

- 6 pits, 4 seeds. Sow anticlockwise into your own store as you pass it,
  never into the other’s.
- The last seed in your own store: move again.
- The last seed in an empty pit of yours: take it and the pit facing it (if
  that has seeds).
- When one side is empty, each side’s seeds go to its owner’s store. A game
  scores the seeds won beyond 24; 40 points win.

Congkak, after Hellier
----------------------

- 7 holes, 7 seeds. Sow clockwise (right to left along your row) into your
  own store, never the other’s.
- The last seed in a hole with seeds: lift them and sow on. In your own
  store: sow again from any hole of yours.
- The last seed in an empty hole ends your turn (“dead”); if the hole is
  yours, take the seeds of the hole facing it.
- When your row is empty, fill it again from your store, 7 to a hole; holes
  you cannot fill are ruined wells, out of play. Who cannot fill a single
  hole has lost.

Pallanguli, after Durai
-----------------------

- 7 holes, 6 seeds. Sow anticlockwise; after the last seed, lift the next
  hole and sow on.
- If the hole after the last seed is empty, take the hole beyond it; the
  move ends. Two empty holes: the move ends with nothing.
- Any hole reaching 4 is a cow: it goes at once to the owner of the hole.
- When the board is empty, refill 6 to a hole from your store; holes you
  cannot fill stay closed for the round. The first move alternates. Who
  cannot fill one hole has lost.

Toguz Kumalak
-------------

- 9 holes, 9 stones. Lift a hole, put the first stone back in it, sow the
  rest anticlockwise. A single stone moves on to the next hole.
- The last stone makes a hole on the other side even: take it.
- The last stone makes a hole on the other side 3: it becomes your tuzdyk,
  and every stone sown into it later is yours. Once a game; not the ninth
  hole; not the hole matching the other’s tuzdyk.
- 82 stones win; 81 each is a draw. A side with no move ends the game, and
  the other takes the stones left.

Bohnenspiel
-----------

- 6 pits, 6 beans. Sow anticlockwise; the stores are not sown into.
- The last bean makes a pit (either side) 2, 4 or 6: take it, and the pits
  before it holding 2, 4 or 6.
- A side with no move ends the game; the beans left go to the other. The
  most beans wins.

Playing it in the terminal
--------------------------

- Left, Right: choose a pit; Enter: sow it; or click a pit.
- Any key or click while seeds move: finish the move.
- Lane’s games, setting out: Left, Right (Tab: the other row) choose a pit;
  Up, Down: a pebble more or less; R: the rest at random; Enter: done. Then
  T turns the board round, Enter keeps it.
- M: menu; F1: this help; Q: quit.
