The Royal Game of Ur
Asciiverse
2026-10-25

A race for two, Light and Dark. Throw, then move one piece that many squares
along your path; the first side to bring every piece home wins. Each side
starts on its own outer row, runs along the shared middle row and goes off
the board. Pieces are taken only on the middle row. The rules of each set in
short follow; the full rules, their sources and the history are in RULES.txt
and the rulebook.

The board
---------

- The Ur board (Finkel 2017, British Museum, Bell): enter on your own row at
  the fourth square from the left and run left (squares 1 to 4), along the
  whole middle row the other way (5 to 12), back into your own row (13, 14),
  then off: bearing off is the 15th step.
- Squares 1 to 4, 13 and 14 are yours alone. Pieces are taken only on the
  middle row.
- Rosettes on squares 4, 8 and 14; square 8, the middle rosette, is the only
  one on the shared row.

The throws
----------

- Four lots (Finkel 2017, British Museum): the marked tips up, 0 to 4, are
  the move. 0 loses the turn.
- Three lots (Bell): 3 tips up, 5; none, 4; two, 1; each moves and then
  throws again. One tip up: no move, and the turn ends.
- Two knucklebones (the tablet): the sheep’s gives 1 to 4. Use it, or dare
  the ox’s: yes turns 1, 2, 3, 4 into 5, 6, 7, 10; no wastes the throw. The
  ox says yes half the time.

Finkel 2017
-----------

- 7 pieces each, off the board. Who throws first is chosen at random.
- Four lots; 0 loses the turn. Any throw brings a new piece on, that many
  squares along.
- Not onto your own piece. Landing on an enemy on the middle row sends it
  back to the start; taking is never forced.
- Landing on any rosette (4, 8, 14): throw again.
- A piece on the middle rosette cannot be taken, so nothing lands there.
- Bear off with the exact throw: from 14 a 1, from 13 a 2. You must move if
  you can. All 7 home wins.

British Museum 1990s
--------------------

- As Finkel 2017, but 5 pieces each.
- No square is safe: a piece on the middle rosette can be taken too.
- Four lots, 0 loses the turn; any throw brings a piece on; any rosette
  gives another throw; exact throw to bear off. All 5 home wins.

R. C. Bell’s betting game
-------------------------

- 7 pieces each. Purses start at 20 and carry on while you keep this rule
  set; each side puts 5 into the pool at the start.
- Three lots: 5, 4 or 1 moves and you throw again, even when it could not be
  used. One tip up ends the turn.
- A piece comes on only with a 5, onto your first square. A taken piece
  needs a 5 again.
- Landing on an enemy on the middle row takes it; no square is safe.
- A rosette gives no extra throw: the opponent pays 1 into the pool.
- Exact throw to bear off. The first with all 7 home takes the pool.

The Babylonian tablet
---------------------

- The later board: houses 1 to 4 on your own row, the 12 houses of the
  middle row (5 to 16), then off. Rosettes on houses 4, 8, 12 and 16.
- 5 birds each, each brought on only by its own throw: Swallow 2 (onto house
  4, the rosette), Storm-bird 5, Raven 6, Rooster 7, Eagle 10 (onto that
  house).
- Throw the sheep’s knucklebone (1 to 4); use it, or press D to dare the
  ox’s: yes makes 5, 6, 7 or 10, no wastes the throw.
- One piece per square. Landing on an enemy on the middle row knocks it off;
  a bird on a rosette cannot be knocked off.
- Purses start at 40 and carry on while you keep this rule set; each side
  puts 5 into the pool. Landing on a rosette wins the bird’s tokens from the
  pool, passing over one pays them in: Swallow 3, Storm-bird, Raven and
  Rooster 4, Eagle 5. No extra throw.
- Exact throw to bear off; you must move if you can (or dare). The first
  with all 5 home takes the pool.

Playing it in the terminal
--------------------------

- Space or Enter: throw (or click).
- Arrows or Tab: choose a lit piece; Enter or Space: move it. Or click a lit
  piece; the tray of waiting pieces brings a new one on.
- D: dare the ox’s knucklebone (the tablet). With no move, Enter lets the
  throw pass.
- Game over: Enter plays again, M goes to the menu.
- M: menu; F1 or ?: this help; Q: quit.
