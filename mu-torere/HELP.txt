Mū tōrere
Asciiverse
2026-10-23

Two players have four men each on an eight-pointed star: eight points
(kewai) round a centre (pūtahi), each kewai joined to its two neighbours and
to the pūtahi. The men start on four neighbouring kewai each, the pūtahi
empty. In turn, each moves one man to the one empty point, if it is joined
to it. Nothing is taken: who cannot move has lost. The rules of each form in
short follow; the full rules, their sources and the history are in RULES.txt
and the rulebook.

The pūtahi beside an enemy
--------------------------

- 4 men each: White on kewai 1 to 4, Black on 5 to 8. White begins.
- A move: a man goes to the empty point, along the ring to the next kewai,
  from a kewai into the pūtahi, or out of the pūtahi to any kewai.
- A man may enter the pūtahi only if one of the kewai beside it holds an
  enemy man, at any time in the game.
- Who cannot move has lost. The same position a third time: a draw.

Mū tōrere after Best
--------------------

- As above, but the rule about the pūtahi holds only for the opening move:
  the first man to move must come from beside an enemy. After that, any man
  may enter the empty pūtahi.
- Read this way the side that moves second can win at once, as the program
  does; see RULES.txt.

Playing it in the terminal
--------------------------

- Arrows: move the cursor; Enter or Space: move the man under it (with one
  empty point, each man has at most one place to go). Or click a man. The
  men that may move are lit.
- When the game is over, Enter plays again.
- M: menu; F1: this help; Q: quit.
