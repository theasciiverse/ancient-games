Tabula
Asciiverse
2026-10-25

The Roman ancestor of backgammon. Each side has fifteen men, all in hand at
the start, and three dice. Each die moves one man that many points along its
way round the board: into the board, round it, and off the far end. A point
with two or more men of one side is closed to the other; a single man (a
blot) is hit when an enemy man lands on it and must start again. You must
use the whole throw if you can. A man may leave the board only when all your
men are in the last six points. The first to bear off all fifteen wins. The
rules in short follow; the full rules, the sources and the history are in
RULES.txt and the rulebook.

Rules every set shares
----------------------

- 15 men each, all in hand at the start; three dice; White throws first (our
  choice).
- Entering: a die of n enters a man on your nth point. Men may enter and men
  on the board may move in the same turn.
- Each die moves one man its number of points; one man may take two or three
  dice in turn, but every point it stops on must be open.
- Two or more men of one side close a point. A single man is hit by an enemy
  landing on it and goes back to hand; while you have a hit man, you must
  enter it before anything else moves.
- Use as many dice as you can; if not all, the highest you can (our choice,
  after Alfonso X). Each die counts once, doubles and triples too (our
  choice).
- Bearing off: only when all your men are in your last six points; a man
  leaves on an exact throw, or a higher one if no man of yours stands
  further back (our choice).

Tabula, the colours opposite
----------------------------

- Contrary motion, as in backgammon: White enters on points 1 to 6 and bears
  off from 19 to 24; Black enters on 24 to 19 and bears off from 6 to 1.

Tabula, the colours the same way
--------------------------------

- Parallel motion: both sides enter on points 1 to 6, run the same way round
  the board and bear off from 19 to 24. Every point is contested from the
  start.

Ludus duodecim scriptorum
-------------------------

- Three rows of twelve, both sides the same way: in along the middle row
  (points 1 to 12), back along the top (13 to 24), along the bottom (25
  to 36) and off.
- No man may go beyond point 12 while any of yours is still in hand.
- Bearing off from the last six points, 31 to 36. Otherwise as tabula.

Playing it in the terminal
--------------------------

- Enter or Space: throw. Then arrows (or the mouse) to a lit man, Enter to
  pick it up, a lit point (or “Borne off”) to put it down; one die at a
  time. Esc: let go, or take back the last step.
- In hand and Borne off stand above and below the board.
- When the game is over, Enter plays again.
- M: menu; F1: this help; Q: quit.
