Morris
Asciiverse
2026-10-23

Two players enter their men one at a time on the points of a board of lines,
then move them along the lines. Three men of one colour in a row along a
line make a mill. In Three Men’s Morris a mill wins; in the larger games
each mill takes one of the other side’s men, and a side left with two men,
or with no move, has lost. The rules of each form in short follow; the full
rules, their sources and the history are in RULES.txt and the rulebook.

Nine Men’s Morris, today’s rules
--------------------------------

- 9 men each. White begins. In turn, each enters a man on any empty point
  until all 18 are in; then each moves one man a turn along a line to the
  next empty point.
- Three of your men on one line make a mill: take one of the other side’s
  men, but not one in a mill, unless all of them are in mills. Two mills at
  once still take one man. A mill opened and closed again takes again.
- Down to three men, a side flies: it moves a man to any empty point.
- A side left with two men, or with no move, loses.
- The same position with the same side to move a third time: a draw.

Nine Men’s Morris after Bell
----------------------------

- As today’s rules, with these differences:
- No flying: a side down to three still moves along the lines.
- A mill never takes a man that stands in a mill; if every one does, it
  takes nothing.
- A side left with two men, or with no move, loses. A side whose only men
  stand in a mill must still move, even out of it.

Morabaraba, Mind Sports South Africa
------------------------------------

- 12 cows (men) each on the board with diagonals: the corners of the three
  squares are joined, and mills run along the diagonals too. Cows move along
  any line, diagonals included.
- A mill shoots (takes) one cow, not one in a mill while there is any other;
  two mills at once still shoot one.
- A cow that left a mill to make a new one may not go straight back on your
  next move if that makes a mill again.
- Down to three cows, a side flies. Two cows left, or no move: lost.
- The board filled with no cow shot: a draw. A side down to three and no cow
  shot in ten moves each: a draw.

Six Men’s Morris after Bell
---------------------------

- 6 men each on a board of two squares (16 points), their sides’ middles
  joined. Mills only along the sides of the squares.
- Enter all men in turn, then move along the lines, as in Nine Men’s Morris.
  No flying.
- A mill takes any one man of the other side, in a mill or not.
- A side left with two men, or with no move, loses.

Three Men’s Morris after Bell
-----------------------------

- 3 men each on 9 points: a square of three by three, joined along the rows,
  the columns and both diagonals. White begins.
- Enter the men in turn on empty points, then move one a turn along a line
  to the next empty point.
- The first to have his three men in a row along a line wins. Nothing is
  taken.
- The same position a third time: a draw. With perfect play the side that
  begins wins.

Three Men’s Morris, placing only
--------------------------------

- 4 counters each on 9 points: a square of three by three, joined along the
  rows and columns, without diagonals. White begins.
- In turn, each places a counter on an empty point. Counters never move.
- Three in a row along a row or a column wins. All eight placed and no row:
  a draw.

Playing it in the terminal
--------------------------

- Arrows: move the cursor; Enter or Space: enter a man on an empty point, or
  pick up a man and put it down on a lit point; Esc: let go. Or click.
- After a mill, the men it may take are lit red: choose one.
- When the game is over, Enter plays again.
- M: menu; F1: this help; Q: quit.
