Patolli
Asciiverse
2026-10-25

A cross painted on a mat, its arms divided into compartments; six stones
each, red and blue; five black beans, each with one white hole. Cast the
beans: the holes showing count 1 to 4, all five 10. Each player brings his
stones in on his own square of the middle and moves them once round the
cross, the way his first stone took, and off beside where they came in. A
compartment holds one stone; nobody is taken. The pinched compartments cost
two forfeits, the rounded ends give another throw. The first with all six
stones off wins the pool. The rules are a reconstruction, R. C. Bell’s; the
chroniclers give only the equipment and the count. The rules in short
follow; the full rules, the sources and the history are in RULES.txt and the
rulebook.

Bell’s suggested rules
----------------------

- Each casts the beans; the higher begins (ties cast again).
- Your first throw brings a stone in on your square of the middle and moves
  it the whole throw, either way round; all your stones then go that way.
  After that a stone comes in only on a throw of 1.
- A throw moves one stone its whole count. No stone may land on a
  compartment that holds a stone (it may pass over). If any stone can move,
  one must.
- No move: a forfeit into the pool. Landing on a pinched compartment: two
  forfeits to the other player. Landing on a rounded end: another throw.
- Stones go once round and off, beside where they came in, by the exact
  throw; the other pays a forfeit for each. The first with all six off wins
  the pool.
- Our choices: a pool of 10 each and purses of 20; a throw of no holes moves
  nothing and costs nothing; a player who cannot pay in full pays what he
  has.

Playing it in the terminal
--------------------------

- Enter or Space: cast the beans. Then arrows (or the mouse) to a lit
  compartment, Enter to send a stone there; B (or the “bear off” button) to
  bear a stone off.
- The panel shows the beans as they fell (◉ a hole up), the purses and the
  pool, and which way each side goes.
- When the game is over, Enter plays again.
- M: menu; F1: this help; Q: quit.
