Seega
Asciiverse
2026-10-25

A square of holes, five, seven or nine a side. Each player has a kelb
(piece) for half the holes but the centre. Two each are set down on fixed
holes; then the players set down two at a time, anywhere but the centre,
until only the centre is empty. Then a move is one step, along a row or a
column, into an empty hole. A kelb that comes to stand so that an enemy kelb
lies between it and another of yours takes that enemy kelb; as long as you
can take again at once, you move again. If you cannot move, the other must
open a way by giving up a kelb of his. The rules in short follow; the full
rules, the source and the history are in RULES.txt and the rulebook.

Seega of five
-------------

- 5 by 5, twelve kelbs each. Stone has kelbs on c1 and c5, Brick on a3 and
  e3. Then Stone sets down two, Brick two, and so on, anywhere but the
  centre c3.
- Brick, who placed last, moves first: a step into the centre. Our choice;
  Lane does not say who begins.
- A move: one step along a row or column into an empty hole.
- Taking: a kelb that steps so that an enemy kelb lies between it and
  another of yours, in a row or column, takes it; several at once if so
  placed. A kelb may step in between two enemies safely.
- After taking, if you can take again at once (with any kelb), you must move
  again, and only to take.
- No move: the other gives up a kelb next to one of yours, and you move.
- A side with no kelbs has lost. Our choice: after 40 moves with nothing
  taken or given, more kelbs win; equal is a draw.

Seega of seven and of nine
--------------------------

- The seb’áwee, 7 by 7, twenty-four kelbs each, and the tis’áwee, 9 by 9,
  forty each. The same rules as on the board of five.
- The first four kelbs stand on the middle holes of the outer rows (Stone)
  and outer columns (Brick), as on Lane’s board of five. Our choice.

Playing it in the terminal
--------------------------

- Arrows: move the cursor; Enter or Space: set a kelb down, give one up, or
  pick one up and put it on a lit hole; Esc: let go. Or click.
- The kelbs a step would take are marked ✕ when the cursor is on where it
  goes.
- When the game is over, Enter plays again.
- M: menu; F1: this help; Q: quit.
