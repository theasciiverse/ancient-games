Fox and Geese
Asciiverse
2026-10-25

One player has a single fox, the other a flock of geese, on a cross of 33
points joined by lines along the rows and columns (no diagonals). The fox
kills geese by jumping them; the geese try to hem him in until he cannot
move. The rules of each form in short follow; the full rules, their sources
and the history are in RULES.txt and the rulebook.

Bell, thirteen geese
--------------------

- 13 geese fill the row below the middle and the bottom arm; the fox starts
  two points above the middle goose. The geese move first.
- One piece a turn, one step along a line to an empty point. Geese may step
  any way.
- The fox may instead jump a goose next to him onto the empty point beyond,
  killing it. He may jump again and again in one move, turning between jumps
  and stopping when he likes. Jumping is never forced.
- The fox cannot move: the geese win. Three geese or fewer left, or the
  geese cannot move: the fox wins.
- The same position with the same side to move a third time: a draw.

Bell, seventeen geese
---------------------

- As thirteen geese, with 17 geese: two more on the outer edge of each side
  arm, just above the long row of geese.
- A goose moves up or sideways, never down towards where the geese started.
  The fox still moves any way.
- Geese left with no move lose: the fox wins.

Playing it in the terminal
--------------------------

- Arrows: move the cursor; Enter or Space: pick up a piece, then put it down
  on a lit point; Esc: let go. Or click a piece, then a point.
- Geese a jump would kill turn red when the cursor is on its landing point.
  Where several series of jumps end on the same point, the one killing the
  most geese is played.
- When the game is over, Enter plays again.
- M: menu; F1: this help; Q: quit.
