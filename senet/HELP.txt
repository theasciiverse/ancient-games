Senet
Asciiverse
2026-10-23

Senet is a race over thirty houses: throw, then move one piece forward that
many houses, and be the first to bring your pieces off the end. Both sides
travel the same way, so pieces block, bump and pass each other. The rules of
each rule set in short follow; the full rules, their sources and the history
are in RULES.txt and the rulebook.

The board
---------

- Houses 1 to 10 run left to right along the top row, 11 to 20 back along
  the middle row, 21 to 30 left to right along the bottom row.
- Kendall’s named houses: 15 Rebirth, 26 Beauty, 27 Water, 28 the Three
  Truths, 29 Re-Atoum, 30 Horus. Bell: 26 is the door, 27 to 30 the marked
  houses.

The throws
----------

- Kendall: four sticks, count the light sides up; none up is 5. After moving
  on a 1, 4 or 5, throw again.
- Bell: a long die, 1 to 4, one throw a turn.
- A throw with no move ends the turn.

Kendall, five pieces
--------------------

- Five each: White on 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, Black on 2, 4, 6, 8, 10; White first.
- Move one piece forward by the throw; you must move if you can. Only if no
  piece can go forward, move one backward.
- Not onto your own piece. A piece with a friend beside it is safe. Landing
  on a lone enemy swaps the two.
- Three or more of a side in a row: the other side may not pass them.
- Every piece must stop on 26. From 26 a 1 falls into the water (27): back
  to 15, or the highest empty house below it; the 1 still throws again.
- 26 bears off on a 5; 28, 29, 30 only on an exact 3, 2, 1.
- First to bear off all five wins.

Kendall, seven pieces
---------------------

- Seven each, alternating on 1 to 14, White on 1; Black first.
- As five pieces, but no blockades, and a lone enemy landed on goes to the
  house you came from.
- Every piece must stop on 26. A piece landing in the water (27) ends the
  turn, and your other pieces are frozen until it is out.
- Out of the water: press R to move it to 15 instead of throwing (not while
  15 is taken; the turn ends), or throw and bear off on a 4.
- A piece in the water is never safe. Landed on in 28 to 30, a piece goes
  into the water, or back to 26 if the water is taken.
- 26 bears off on a 5, 28 on a 3, 29 on a 2, 30 on any throw. Your last
  piece, stuck on 28 or 29, falls back into an empty water.
- First to bear off all seven wins.

R. C. Bell 1960
---------------

- Ten men each on houses 1 to 20; a coin picks who starts, and the starter’s
  men stand on the even houses.
- A long die, 1 to 4, one move a turn, forward only. Not onto your own man.
- Landing on an enemy man captures it; it leaves the game. Men on the
  door (26) and the marked houses (27 to 30) cannot be taken.
- A man on 26 to 30 must move at the first throw that allows it, before any
  other move. From 26 it goes onto an empty marked house.
- From 27 a 4 bears off, from 28 a 3, from 29 a 2, from 30 a 1.
- When the board is empty, the most men borne off wins; equal is a draw.

Playing it in the terminal
--------------------------

- Space or Enter: throw; or click.
- Arrows or Tab: choose a piece; Enter: move it; or click a lit piece.
- R: rescue a piece from the water (Kendall, seven pieces).
- After the game, Enter plays again.
- M: menu; F1: this help; Q: quit.
